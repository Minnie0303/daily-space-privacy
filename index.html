Daily Space Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Daily Space is a personal productivity, study, mood, and diary application. This policy explains what information Daily Space processes, why it is used, and how users can delete it.

Information processed

When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process authentication information such as your Google account identifier and email address, and may process basic profile information used for authentication.

Daily Space may store information you enter into the app, including tasks, labels, due dates and times, study subjects, study sessions, study notes, goals, important dates, mood selections, diary text, weather selections, symptom entries, color/settings choices, and older compatibility data from earlier versions if present.

How information is used

Your information is used to provide the app's features, save your entries, authenticate your account, and synchronize your Daily Space data across devices when you are signed in.

Service providers

Daily Space uses Google for Google sign-in and Supabase for authentication, database hosting, and cloud synchronization. These providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, and authenticate their services.

Sale, advertising, and unrelated sharing

Daily Space does not sell your personal information and does not use your diary, mood, symptom, task, or study entries for advertising. Data is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the functions described above, unless disclosure is required by law.

Local storage and security

Daily Space keeps a local copy of app data on your device and, when cloud sync is enabled, a cloud copy in Supabase. Network connections use HTTPS. Supabase Row Level Security is configured so authenticated users can access their own Daily Space state. Local app storage and user-exported backup text are not encrypted by Daily Space.

Retention and deletion

Cloud account data is retained while your Daily Space account exists. You can permanently delete your Daily Space account and associated cloud data from Account → Delete account & cloud data in the app. The current device's local Daily Space copy is also cleared by that in-app deletion flow. Deleting Daily Space does not delete your Google account.

Backup copies that you manually exported or shared outside Daily Space are controlled by you and cannot be deleted automatically by the app.

For an external account-deletion option, see Delete your Daily Space account.

Your choices

You may use Daily Space without Google cloud sync for local-only use, sign out of cloud sync, export a manual backup, or delete your account and cloud data.
