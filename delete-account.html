Delete your Daily Space account

Daily Space users can permanently delete their Daily Space account and associated cloud data.

Delete from the app

Open Daily Space, sign in if necessary, then go to Account → Delete account & cloud data. Confirm the permanent deletion. This deletes the Daily Space authentication account and associated Daily Space cloud data. It does not delete your Google account.

If you cannot access the app

Email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL with the subject Daily Space account deletion request. We may need to verify that you control the account before deleting it.

What is deleted

The Daily Space account and associated cloud-synced Daily Space state, including tasks, study data, mood/diary data, symptom entries, labels/settings, and other synced app data associated with that account.

What is not deleted automatically

Your Google account is not deleted. Backup text or copies that you exported outside Daily Space are not controlled by the app and cannot be removed automatically.
